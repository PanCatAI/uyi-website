1. Scope and operator

This policy explains how personal information is handled in the UYI website, experience-update emails, macOS desktop application, UYI Studio and related creation features. Product accounts currently require an invitation.

The operator is the Guangzhou Branch of Harness Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd. (哈尔尼斯（北京）科技有限公司广州分公司), China ("we", "us" or "our"). Our Guangzhou operations team manages website signups and experience-update emails. Our engineering team develops and maintains the application, accounts and model services and handles troubleshooting.

2. Website and email signup

Joining the experience-update email list is optional. We use your email address, signup time, display language, source and submission-management information to register you, handle repeated submissions, send experience announcements and development updates, and respond to signup requests. Email signups are managed separately from application login accounts.

Cloudflare delivers the website and processes signup requests. Signup records are stored in Feishu Bitable and viewed by our operations personnel in Guangzhou. Cloudflare may process technical information, such as IP addresses, for delivery and protection.

The website stores your display-language preference in browser local storage. Clearing site data resets that preference but does not also delete email signups, product accounts, diagnostic reports or on-device chats and memory. When you contact us, your return address and message are used to respond or handle your request.

3. Accounts, authentication and usage

Resend sends verification codes for email login. With Google login, the account identifier and email returned after authentication are associated with your UYI account. Accounts, device sessions, acceptance times and policy versions support authentication, login management and agreement records.

Operational information, such as capabilities used, request identifiers, processing status, times and usage, supports request processing, monthly allowances, abuse response and troubleshooting. Relaying online content and retaining it in logs are separate operations.

Desktop access tokens are valid for 15 minutes. Refresh tokens remain valid for seven days from each successful refresh. These are authentication-validity periods, not deadlines for deleting account information or logs.

4. Chat, character actions and memory

Online responses send the current message and relevant conversation context to a model. When autonomous activity is enabled, current goals or room state may be sent to a decision model to determine the character’s next action even without a new message from you. Conversation and action decisions can use different services.

Chat history and character memory are stored on your device. UYI does not currently provide cloud backups of these two types of data. However, using an online model to organise or retrieve memory sends the query and relevant candidate-memory text. Memories included in conversation context also become model inputs.

Chats and derived memories are stored separately. Deleting a chat may leave memories created from it on your device. Switching between basic memory and UYI memory in Settings → Memory does not delete all data.

5. Voice, images and files

Online speech recognition sends microphone audio. Online speech synthesis sends the text to read and the selected voice. Real-time voice conversation sends audio and conversation instructions and receives reply audio and transcripts. These operations support recognition, responses and speech output.

Push-to-talk starts recording when pressed and ends that recording when released. Continuous voice mode uses the microphone across exchanges; you can exit that mode. Revoking microphone permission in macOS prevents the application from continuing to capture microphone audio. Typing remains available.

The room camera captures the virtual room. Asset import uses the files or directories you select for the relevant creation and display features. Virtual-room photography and reading the computer screen are different operations. Screens, audio and files can contain information about other people; avoid sending unnecessary personal or confidential information. Revoking an OS permission does not delete information already transmitted.

6. Online services and local models

For online features routed through UYI, the UYI API receives inputs and relevant context and forwards them to model or speech services. Hosted speech currently uses Qwen services and hosted inference uses DeepSeek services. Alibaba Cloud (aliyun) supplies cloud infrastructure and diagnostic-attachment storage.

User-configured voice options include ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and OpenAI-compatible connections. Inference supports OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic-compatible connections; 3D generation supports Meshy-compatible and Tripo-compatible connections. A compatible-interface name does not mean a request necessarily reaches that brand’s official service. Inputs for the relevant feature go to the endpoint you configure. External 3D generation receives prompts and selected reference materials.

Irodori TTS for local speech and motionbricks or SOMA for local motion perform the relevant processing on your device. Settings apply per capability: choosing local speech synthesis does not make online-configured chat local. Login, model downloads and voluntary diagnostic submission also require network access. UYI-hosted cloud 3D generation and motion services are under development.

Conditions for an online service’s retention, evaluation, training or human review of inputs and outputs depend on the service, contract and settings. The statement that UYI’s reported statistics are not used for training is not a guarantee about all content received by model services. Check the terms and privacy information of services you connect yourself as well.

7. Usage statistics and voluntary diagnostics

We use PostHog for product-usage event statistics. Reported data is used for debugging and statistics, not training. Statistical reporting and problem reports are separate processes and seek consent separately. Records linked to accounts or identifiers are distinct from aggregate results that cannot identify an individual.

A problem report sends your description, platform, application version, request identifier and any diagnostic files you choose to attach. Personnel authorised to handle diagnostics review them to investigate and address faults. Do not include unnecessary personal information, credentials or confidential material in descriptions or attachments.

Diagnostic reports and attachments are retained for 30 days and deleted after expiry. While logged in, you can withdraw your own reports earlier. Withdrawal clears the description and associated request identifier and deletes attachments. Previously issued temporary download links may nevertheless remain usable until they expire.

8. Saving and sharing creations

Diaries written by characters are stored in the virtual room on your device. “Publishing” a diary inside the room does not publish it on the internet. Exporting a scene initially creates a local file.

Giving an exported file to someone else or uploading it to an external platform is a separate action you choose. Check the personal information included and the audience before sharing. External-service processing is subject to that service’s privacy information. Deletion by UYI alone cannot remove copies saved or forwarded by third parties.

9. Service providers, overseas processing and security

To provide the services, Cloudflare, Feishu, Google, Resend, Alibaba Cloud, the statistics environment using PostHog and the selected model services process information in their respective roles described above. UYI processing is not confined to Japan: personnel in Guangzhou, China, view email-signup information. Other processing and storage locations depend on the service and configuration; this policy does not promise Japan-only storage.

Where overseas provision of personal data requires particular information or consent under applicable law, that information and consent are required. Reading this policy or continuing to use the service does not replace such separate consent.

Account administration, troubleshooting and diagnostic access depend on personnel permissions. Technical response measures include investigation by request identifier, device-session revocation, diagnostic-attachment withdrawal and review of administrative action records. Applicable law governs security, oversight of service providers, and investigation, remediation, reporting and notification following incidents.

10. Retention, logout and account deletion

Email addresses provided for website experience updates are retained for 12 months. You may request deletion of signup details or unsubscribe earlier by writing from the registered address to partnerships@uyi.live. Section 7 describes the 30-day retention and withdrawal of diagnostic reports and attachments.

Logging out ends the current login. Account deletion disables the account and revokes login access while retaining a deletion marker and administrative action records. It does not physically erase every associated record. Local chats and memory, and information sent to online services, are also handled separately.

We do not currently publish fixed deletion periods by category for account records, usage statistics, service logs and infrastructure backups. The 12 months for email signup, 30 days for diagnostics and 15-minute or seven-day authentication periods are not common retention periods for those records. Retention remains limited by each purpose and applicable law; this explanation does not authorise indefinite retention or new uses.

11. Your choices and requests

For access, correction or deletion of website signup information, or to unsubscribe, contact partnerships@uyi.live. Writing from the original signup address helps verify your identity. Tell us the action you want, without sending unrelated identity documents or sensitive information.

Signed-in users can review and withdraw their diagnostic reports and revoke device login access. Stopping emails, disabling an account, withdrawing a report and deleting on-device memory are different procedures.

Applicable law may give you rights to notice of purposes, access to retained personal data or third-party provision records, correction, cessation of use, deletion and stopping third-party provision. The signup contact and self-service operations described above do not limit statutory rights to signup data or those operations. We remain responsible for personal-information management and responding to individuals’ requests.

12. Minors and changes to this policy

Obtain guardian consent before registering or using the service where applicable law requires it. Guardians may request that emails stop or that an address registered without required consent be deleted. This does not represent that the application has implemented age or guardian verification.

We update this policy as information handling changes and show the update date on the website. Material changes, including changes in purposes or recipients, will be communicated appropriately, with separate information and consent procedures where required by law. Future features such as cloud storage of chat or memory will also require an explanation of the information involved and applicable conditions.

Publishing this policy alone does not establish past consent or mean every user has agreed to new processing.
