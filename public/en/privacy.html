Privacy Policy (draft)

Draft version 2026-11-18

This policy explains how we handle information when you explore the UYi website, sign up for early access emails or contact us. You can stop receiving updates at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your information

The Guangzhou branch of Harness Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd. (哈尔尼斯（北京）科技有限公司广州分公司) operates this website and is responsible for signup data. Its business address is 广州市海珠区新港东路工美港15号楼. The person in charge of the branch is 段若阳. For privacy requests, write to partnerships@uyi.live.

2. Exploring the room and following links

You can explore the room without signing up. In the current preview, character reactions, music choices and mouse interactions run in your browser. Your browser remembers the display language you select. Clearing site data resets this preference. If you follow a link to X or Discord, that platform’s privacy policy applies to what you do there.

3. What we use when you sign up or write to us

Signing up is optional. The only field you fill in is your email address. By submitting it, you ask to receive UYi early access announcements and occasional development updates. If you write to us, we use your return address and message to respond or handle your request. Please leave out identity documents or sensitive details unrelated to your request.

4. Signup storage and service providers

We accept email signups on this website. Cloudflare hosts the site and processes signup requests. We store your email address and signup details, such as the registration time, display language and source, in Feishu Bitable so the operator can manage the list and send updates. We use a request identifier to recognise repeated submissions. Cloudflare may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service.

Feishu Privacy Policy · Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Access, disclosure and processing across borders

The operator in Guangzhou, China, accesses the list to manage signups and send emails. Signup information is processed through Feishu and Cloudflare and may be handled outside your country of residence. We limit use and disclosure to what is needed to manage signups, handle requests or comply with legal obligations. Contact us about the services used and the cross-border arrangements that apply to your signup.

6. How long we keep information and how we protect it

We have yet to adopt a retention period for signup records. We propose deleting them within 12 months of signup, or sooner if these announcements end. This would cover signup records stored in Feishu, exported lists and any saved notification emails. We limit access to people handling recruitment, email updates and privacy requests. We keep correspondence only as long as we need it to handle the matter, unless the law requires longer retention.

7. How to unsubscribe or exercise your rights

Email partnerships@uyi.live with the address you used and the action you want us to take. You may unsubscribe, withdraw consent to updates, or ask to access, copy, correct or delete your information. Unsubscribing stops recruitment and development emails. You may also ask us to erase the signup record. If verification is needed, we will ask only for information necessary to check the address. We will explain any legal limits on fulfilling your request.

8. Children’s privacy and changes to this policy

Where applicable law requires a guardian’s consent, please obtain it before signing up. Guardians may contact us to stop emails and delete a child’s address registered by mistake or without the required consent. We will date revisions posted on this website. Before making a material change to our purposes or recipients, we will explain the change and seek fresh consent where the law requires it.
